IPL Ticket Booking Guidelines

Ticket Verification

Carry your valid IPL match ticket while entering the stadium. Make sure your ticket QR code or barcode is clearly visible for verification.

ID Proof Requirement

Carry a valid government-issued photo ID proof along with your ticket. The ID details may be checked at the stadium entrance.

Prohibited Items

Do not carry outside food, bottles, sharp objects, fireworks, or other restricted items inside the stadium.
